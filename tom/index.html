The main engagement

Strategy & Execution Intensive

Most companies don't fail for lack of ideas. They fail because the strategy is fuzzy and the execution drifts. The Intensive fixes both, in that order, for your company and your product.

1 · Strategy Intensive

About one quarter

We interrogate the team and the market until the answers are written down: where you will win, for whom, and why they'll choose you. Markets and segments, problems to solve, ideal customer profile, competitive advantage, pricing, sales and distribution.

2 · Execution Intensive

One to two quarters

Strategy only pays off when it ships. We align your functions around it: go-to-market, product sequencing, pricing rollout, messaging, sales enablement, onboarding, and the operating cadence that keeps everyone honest.

3 · Continuous

Ongoing

Markets move and plans meet reality. A fortnightly deep call, with texts in between, keeps the strategy current and the execution relentless.

Who it's for

Founders and leadership teams who have real traction or real funding, and know the next stage needs a sharper strategy, not just more effort.

Honest expectations

For a full strategy reset, plan on six months at minimum. Twelve months is the sweet spot. You'll see evidence that it's working well before that.

Founder Success

Product Leadership
