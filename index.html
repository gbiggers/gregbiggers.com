Transformation

Transforming to the Product Operating Model

Starting with the executives, and quickly executing through the teams, I lead growth stage companies through transformation to the product operating model.

It is massive work to shift from outputs to outcomes. Work that is worth it to achieve breakthrough results.

But— the move to continuous product discovery and delivery is not enough. Too many organizations do not embrace the hard work of also transforming their market and product strategy. Identifying where we win, and how to exploit our unique advantages to deliver what the market needs next.

I help my transformation clients navigate high-stakes choices, pairing smart strategy with continuous execution by teams on the ground to deliver breakthrough success.
