Product Strategy and Execution Intensives

“A joy to work with. Greg helped the team really prioritize market needs and securing new sales. Highly respected by my entire team.”

—Chief Revenue Officer

Strategy

Get the strategy fundamentals right—everything else flows from there.

During the Strategy Intensive, we interrogate the team and the market to discover and deliver fresh strategic imperatives for the company and product.

Guiding with strong analysis and evidence, we help you produce a written strategy covering your company’s answers to fundamental questions about where and how you will win:

  • Markets and Market Segments

  • Problems to Solve

  • Ideal Customer Profile

  • Solutions to Deliver

  • Competitive Advantage and Differentiation

  • Value and Pricing

  • Sales and Distribution Models

  • Customer Success Implementation

Execution

Strategy only pays off when it ships—execution is where strategy turns into revenue.

As the evidence-based strategy solidifies, we partner with you to align your functional departments, kicking off the Execution Intensive.

Working across company functions, we guide and coach focused, relentless execution.

For most companies, the Execution Intensive includes:

  • Go-to-Market Alignment

  • Product Development Sequencing

  • Messaging and Positioning

  • Website, Pitch Decks, and Demos

  • Pricing and Packaging Rollout

  • Sales and Distribution Channels

  • Sales Enablement and Training

  • Customer Onboarding and Success Playbooks

  • Operating Cadence and Metrics

Intensive

Strategy and execution aren’t a quarterly event—they’re a continuous rhythm.

Each intensive is a concentrated burst of focused work with your leadership team: dedicated sessions, hard questions, and decisions made in the room.

The intensives give you an aligned strategy and a running start. But nothing here is ever one-and-done. Markets move, customers change, and plans meet reality. Strategy keeps adapting to what’s happening on the ground, and execution becomes continuous: always intense, and always acting on up-to-date strategy decisions.

Throughout, coaching continues: a fortnightly deep call, with texts in between, to keep the strategy honest and execution relentless.

ContinuousFounder Success

Founder Success Coaching

“A great coach who really delivers. I would follow Greg into the darkness.”

—Executive Coach

Founders don’t need more advice—they need a partner who has sat in the chair. A cool approach to the hot seat.

Founder Success is ongoing coaching for founders from seed to Series C: a fortnightly deep call, with texts in between, focused on whatever is holding the company back.

With most founders, we work on:

  • Product-Market Fit: who has the most pressing need, why they buy, and how to find more of them

  • ICP: an ideal customer profile built on evidence, not hope

  • Scaling: growing repeatable sales and distribution motions

  • Breaking the founder bottleneck: decision making, velocity, and growing who owns what

When it’s time to reset the strategy or realign the team, we add the Strategy and Execution Intensives.

Product Leadership Coaching

“Greg helps us collaborate with executive stakeholders better than anyone I’ve seen.”

—Founder, VP Engineering

Taste is not enough—in the age of AI, the difference is judgment.

AI can make building faster and options endless. Taste can help us get attractiveness and desirability right. But deciding what to ship, what to stop, and what will actually change customer and business outcomes requires judgment honed by experience: clarity, speed, and new kinds of collaboration between Product, Design, and Engineering.

Moving from outputs to outcomes is the supporting work. Starting with the executives, and quickly proving it with pilot teams, I coach product leaders and lead growth-stage companies through the transformation to the product operating model. For most product leaders, we work on:

  • Judgment: deciding what to build, and what not to

  • Clarity: sharper strategy, priorities, and decisions

  • Speed: shortening the path from insight to shipped outcome

  • New ways of collaborating with Design and Engineering

  • Outputs to outcomes: changing how the organization measures success

  • Leading the transformation: executives first, pilot teams next

Interim / Fractional Chief Product Officer

Need product leadership without another full-time executive? I can help on a fractional or interim basis: try a CPO before you commit, cover a gap while yours is out, or add strategic oversight without the disruption of a new hire.

Product Career Jumpstart

“Greg is the best coach I’ve ever seen. Totally upgraded my personal firmware.”

—VP Product

The jump to VP or CPO is a different job—not a bigger version of the old one. Are you ready for the next chair?

Product Career Jumpstart is for senior product managers and directors preparing for or stepping up to VP or CPO: how to lead leaders, own strategy, and earn the room. High-value topics covered usually (tailored to the coachee) include:

  • Leading through other leaders, not around them

  • Owning product strategy, not just the roadmap

  • Partnering with the CEO, the board, and peers in sales, marketing, and engineering

  • Positioning yourself for the next role

  • Your first 90 days in the new role

You know you have more leadership potential—you just need help unlocking it.
